## Research

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/wave_modl.png" class="research-thumb" alt="Wave-MoDL">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Deep Learning Based Image Reconstruction</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Model-based deep learning (MoDL) folds CNN regularizers into physics-based parallel imaging with few parameters. We extend it with wave encoding — Wave-MoDL — combining unrolled network constraints with data consistency for highly accelerated, high-fidelity 3D imaging.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/qmri.png" class="research-thumb" alt="Quantitative MRI">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Rapid Quantitative MRI</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Quantitative MRI (qMRI) estimates tissue relaxation parameters — T1, T2, T2*, and proton density — in absolute units. It reveals changes that qualitative imaging misses, but high-resolution qMRI is slow. We accelerate acquisition while preserving the accuracy of the quantitative maps.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/dmri.png" class="research-thumb" alt="Diffusion MRI">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Motion-Robust Multi-Shot Diffusion MRI</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Echo-planar imaging (EPI) is fast but distortion-prone; multi-shot EPI reduces distortion and blurring at the cost of inter-shot phase and motion sensitivity. We develop efficient, motion-robust, distortion-free high-resolution msEPI methods, including VUDU and BUDA.</p>
</div>
