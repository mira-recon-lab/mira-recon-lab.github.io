### About the lab

MIRA Lab, led by Dr. Jaejin Cho in the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics at Sejong University, builds methods at the intersection of MRI physics and deep learning. We aim for fast, high-fidelity acquisition and reconstruction that translates directly to clinical and neuroscientific use — from accelerated quantitative mapping to distortion-free, motion-robust diffusion imaging.
