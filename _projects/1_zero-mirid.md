**Zero-MIRID** (zero-shot self-supervised learning of Multi-shot Image Reconstruction for Improved Diffusion MRI) jointly reconstructs multi-shot diffusion-weighted EPI data by incorporating self-supervised deep learning-based image regularization techniques.

- 📄 Paper: [MICCAI 2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-43907-0_44)
- 💻 Code: [github.com/jaejin-cho/miccai2023](https://github.com/jaejin-cho/miccai2023)
