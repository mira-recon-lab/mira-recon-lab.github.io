**Wave-MoDL** (wave-encoded model-based deep learning for image reconstruction) enables highly accelerated MR scans that improve image fidelity and acquisition speed using a wave-encoding strategy combined with a deep learning network.

- 📄 Paper: [Bioengineering 9.12 (2022): 736](https://doi.org/10.3390/bioengineering9120736) — _Editor's pick_
