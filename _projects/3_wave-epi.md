**Wave-EPI** (wave-encoded echo-planar imaging) enables highly accelerated single- and multi-shot EPI with reduced g-factor and artifacts. It may facilitate clinical and neuroscientific applications of EPI by improving spatial and temporal resolution in functional and diffusion imaging.

- 📄 Paper: [MRM 88.3 (2022): 1180-1197](https://doi.org/10.1002/mrm.29291)
- 💻 Code: [github.com/jaejin-cho/wave-EPI](https://github.com/jaejin-cho/wave-EPI)
